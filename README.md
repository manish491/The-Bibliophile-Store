# 📚 The Bibliophile Store

Your online bookstore for new and used books, at affordable prices.

🌐 **Visit us:** https://thebibliophilestore.vercel.app

## About us

The Bibliophile Store is for people who love books. We sell new and used books, and **every book is just ₹150**.

## What we offer

- New and used books across many categories
- Online delivery all over India through India Post
- Easy ordering on WhatsApp
- Book requests: tell us the book you want and we will try to find it
- A WhatsApp reading community for book lovers

## How to order

1. Browse the books on our website.
2. Add your favourite books to your order.
3. Send the order to us on WhatsApp.
4. We pack it and deliver it to your door.

*A book in your hand is a world you can always return to.*
